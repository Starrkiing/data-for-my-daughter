## Basics for Data

Dandy's World is a multiplayer survival game on Roblox, made by BlushCrunch Studio. It has a spooky cartoon-mascot theme.

Players work together as characters called Toons. They finish machines as they travel farther down into Gardenview Center.

Different Toons have different abilities and stats. Players can also collect Trinkets to change how they play.
