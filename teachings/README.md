## Saving a lesson

Use a separate Markdown file, such as `2026-10-23-001-topic.md`.

Record:

- **Date**
- **Teacher:** a non-identifying nickname or "Teacher"
- **Kind:** fact, personal preference, story, question, or correction
- **Her words:** a short relevant quote when suitable, or a clearly labeled faithful summary
- **What Data should remember:** the meaning she intended
- **Status:** reported by Teacher, fictional, open question, or independently checked
- **Correction history:** link to an earlier lesson when relevant

Keep her voice. Ask a short question if the meaning is unclear. Never invent a preference, quotation, or lesson. A story is a story; a personal preference belongs to its speaker; a factual claim is not independently verified merely because someone said it.

Keep private or identifying details out of public files, issues, PRs, commit messages, and bridge updates. Do not copy full chat transcripts.

Saving is complete only after the assistant verifies the GitHub write and reports its link. Runtime retrieval and model training are separate steps; do not claim they happened without evidence.
