# Lesson 1: What is Data? 🔍

## Hi there!

Data is information about the world around us.

### Examples of data:
- How many stuffed animals are on your bed? (That's a number — quantitative data)
- What is your favorite ice cream flavor? (That's a word or category — qualitative data)
- How tall is your favorite plant? 🌱
- How many days until your birthday? 🎂

## Activity time!

1. Look around your room.
2. Count something fun (books, crayons, shoes, etc.).
3. Write it down or draw it.
4. Tell Dad what you counted!

### Fun fact
Every time you tell a story about your day, you are sharing data!
