# Activity: Count My Day 📅

Let's collect some data about your day!

## Instructions
1. Pick one thing to count today. Examples:
   - How many times you smile 😊
   - How many books you look at 📚
   - How many times you say "Dad" 👨
   - How many colors you see outside 🌈

2. Use tally marks or a simple table:

| Time | Count |
|------|-------|
| Morning | |
| Afternoon | |
| Evening | |

3. At the end of the day, total them up!

4. Draw a simple bar chart or just tell the story of your numbers.

### Bonus
Compare with yesterday or with Dad's count. Who had more smiles?

Have fun collecting data! 🎉
